# Qianwen read-only policy

Allowed:

- Public product, merchant, hotel, flight, attraction, map, restaurant, policy, and platform-rule research.
- Public discovery across Taobao, Fliggy, Amap, and Ele.me signals.
- Price, booking-rule, refund-policy, transport, promotion, and market comparison research when no transaction is executed.

Not allowed through the shared family service:

- Ordering, booking, purchasing, payment, transfer, recharge, withdrawal, refund execution, coupon redemption, account binding, or account changes.
- Retrieval of any person's Alipay, social-security, tax, housing-fund, identity, passport, vehicle, property, banking, or other private official records.
- Treating the word “public” as permission to retrieve a named person's protected records.

When a request mixes public policy research with an account or personal-record action, keep only the public policy research and clearly reject the action.
